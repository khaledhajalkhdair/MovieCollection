No. 001

Wet Hot American Summer

2001 · David Wain

●●●●●

A great, infinitely rewatchable film with a surprisingly stacked cast.

"Well I hope it's not jumbo shrimp since I'm allergic to oxymorons!"

No. 002

Burn After Reading

2008 · Joel and Ethan Coen

●●●●●

A very star-studded cast with a very hectic, darkly comedic script and Coen brothers direction. Hilarious and features what is probably my favorite Brad Pitt performance.

As funny as a dark comedy can get without beating you over the head with it or getting too nonsensical.

No. 003

Raising Arizona

1987 · Joel and Ethan Coen

●●●●●

Probably my favorite film of all time. Just a beautiful, sweet ride that doesn’t overstay its welcome and leaves you begging for more.

An absolute cartoon of a film.

No. 004

Inglorious Basterds

2001 · Quentin Tarantino

●●●●●

While some cast decisions have somewhat soured parts of this film for me (Eli Roth in particular), I still consider this one of my favorite films. Each performance is gripping, and the film manages to balance moments of extreme tension with levity and comeuppance.

Pure catharsis in film form.

Django Unchained

●●●●●

Jamie Foxx shooting racist dudes for almost 3 hours? That’s all you had to say.

Even more catharsis in film form. I miss this Tarantino.

No. 007

Tropic Thunder

2008 · Ben Stiller

●●●●○

Funny movie, though I am unsure if I prefer the director’s cut (which I believe is the default viewing option on the 4K disc). I seem to recall not particularly enjoying any of its additions.

No. 008

Heat

1995 · Michael Mann

●●●●●

A beautiful film that offers up a gripping, action-filled heist story, as well as interesting character explorations (particularly in Mccauley and Hanna).

The 4K disc of this bad boy is a beautiful watch. I love Michael Mann's direction.

No. 009

The Raid (4K Restoration)

2011 · Gareth Evans

●●●●●

An incredibly well-made action film. The 4K restoration of this film comes with director-approved color corrections (as well as other bonuses such as director’s commentary and different soundtrack options), making it the best way to watch the film.

I love the changes made to the film's color here; the action feels a lot clearer, while not sacrificing the film's grungy vibe.

No. 010

The Raid 2

2014 · Gareth Evans

●●●●●

Both of the Raid films are some of the finest pieces of action cinema ever created. The Raid 1 is a lean, focused film taking place in one location, while the Raid 2 goes in the extreme opposite direction, instead opting to craft a sprawling crime epic. Both films feature brutal, well-choreographed pencak silat (an Indonesian martial art).

This film, due to its larger budget and greater use of locations, could really do with a 4K restoration as well. That being said, its visuals are already much clearer and brighter than the first Raid's.

No. 011

High School Musical 2

2011 · Kenny Ortega

●●●●○

A musical classic with oh-so-many memorable tracks and musical numbers. I Don’t Dance is one of my favorite musical songs of all time.

Probably my favorite High School Musical film.

No. 012

2 Fast 2 Furious

2003 · John Singleton

●●●○○

A product of its time in all of the best ways and immensely quotable. “Ejecto seato, cuz!” and “I said forget about it, cuh” have both become permanent fixtures of my vocabulary, and the film never takes itself too seriously (though it never quite goes to the lengths of lunacy the later installments do).

No. 013

Spider-Man: Across the Spiderverse

2023 · Varied

●●●●●

One of my favorite Spider-Man movies (Spider-Man also happens to be one of my favorite superheroes). A thrilling, beautiful, vibrant, and defiant piece of animation that feels an hour shorter than its runtime.

Made with so much love (and overworking of the animators, unfortunately) for the character that it is hard not to admire.
